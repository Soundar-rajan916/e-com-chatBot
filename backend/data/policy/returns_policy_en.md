# Store Return, Refund, and Shipping Policies

## Returns and refund window
We offer a 7-day return window from the date of order delivery. If you are not satisfied with your purchase, you may initiate a return, size exchange, or refund request within 7 calendar days after delivery. Once the 7-day return window has elapsed, orders are no longer eligible for return or refund. All returned garments must be unworn, unwashed, unaltered, and with original brand tags, labels, and packaging intact.

## Refund amounts and approval
Refund requests are evaluated based on the order value. Refund amounts of INR 2,000 or less (up to ₹2,000) are auto-approved and processed immediately through our automated system. Refund requests for amounts above INR 2,000 (greater than ₹2,000) require manual verification and approval by a customer support executive before processing.

## Refund timelines and mode
Approved refunds are credited directly to the original payment method used during checkout (such as UPI, credit/debit card, or net banking). The refund amount will reflect in your account within 5 to 7 business days depending on your bank's settlement cycle. For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, refunds are transferred via direct bank transfer or UPI within 5 to 7 business days after details are verified.

## Non-returnable items
Certain categories cannot be returned due to hygiene, customization, and safety standards:
1. Innerwear, undershirts, and socks once their sealed packaging has been opened.
2. Custom-tailored, hemmed, monogrammed, or personalized garments.
3. Traditional pure silk dhotis, angavastrams, or wedding silk sets that have been worn, unpleated, water-stained, or show perfume and scent marks.
4. Men's grooming items, colognes, and fragrances with broken protective seals.
5. Footwear showing scuffed soles, worn outsoles, or creased leather from outdoor use (shoes should be tried on clean carpeted indoor surfaces only).

## Damaged or wrong item
If you receive a defective garment, incorrect size/color, damaged fabric, or broken accessory, report it within 48 hours of delivery along with clear photographs of the product, stitching flaw or defect, original packaging, and courier shipping label. Our team will verify the claim and arrange an immediate free replacement or full refund including reverse pickup.

## Order cancellation
You can cancel an order only before shipping has commenced (while the status is placed or processing). Once an order has been shipped or marked out for delivery, it cannot be cancelled online. If you no longer want the package after shipping, you may refuse delivery at your doorstep when the courier attempts delivery.

## Shipping and delivery timelines
Standard delivery typically takes 2 to 4 business days within Tamil Nadu and major South Indian cities, and 4 to 7 business days for the rest of India. Express shipping options deliver within 24 to 48 hours in metro locations. Orders placed before 2:00 PM are dispatched on the same business day.

## Festival-sale delays
During major festival seasons and annual shopping sales (such as Diwali, Pongal, and Wedding Season sales), dispatch and delivery times may experience delays of 2 to 3 additional business days due to heavy shipping volumes and courier backlogs. Tracking details will continue to be updated in real time.

## Payments and COD
We accept major UPI apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Visa, Mastercard, RuPay credit and debit cards, and Net Banking across all Indian banks. Cash on Delivery (COD) is available for orders up to INR 5,000. For orders above INR 5,000, secure online prepaid payment is required.

You may also request human escalation in this chat at any time.
